Acrobat by Tony portable mode

While this file sits next to pdfcraft.exe, Acrobat by Tony keeps its settings, log files, crash-recovery
autosaves and new digital IDs in the Acrobat by Tony Data folder beside it, and writes nothing to
%APPDATA% or %LOCALAPPDATA%. Copy the whole folder to move Acrobat by Tony with its settings.

Delete this file to use the normal per-user folders (%APPDATA%\Acrobat by Tony and
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Acrobat by Tony) instead. If Acrobat by Tony Data can't be written (for example on a read-only
drive), Acrobat by Tony says so and uses the per-user folders instead.
